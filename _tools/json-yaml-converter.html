{% comment %} Worth being upfront about. The reader here is a subset, not a full YAML implementation, and the two places it will surprise someone are a stream of several documents and the 1.1 booleans — `yes` and `off` are strings under the schema every current parser uses, which is not what people expect. {% endcomment %}

Plain scalars are resolved against the YAML 1.2 core schema, so yes, off and 2024-01-01 stay strings while true, 0x1f and 1e3 do not. Anchors, aliases, merge keys and block scalars are all read; a stream holding more than one document is not, because JSON has nowhere to put the second one.
