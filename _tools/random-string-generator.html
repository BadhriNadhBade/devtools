Output

Generated with crypto.getRandomValues and rejection sampling, so every character and every word in the alphabet is equally likely — no modulo bias. The guessing time assumes an offline attack at ten billion guesses a second against a fast hash; against a slow one like Argon2 it is far longer, and against a leaked plaintext database it is nothing at all. Nothing generated here is stored, remembered or sent anywhere.
